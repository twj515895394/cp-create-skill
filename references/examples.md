# Structural Examples

These examples demonstrate planning logic. They are not mandatory templates.

## Example A — 15-second quick sweet Vlog

Goal:
one compact emotional idea.

Possible structure:
- immediate couple entry,
- shared action,
- playful reaction,
- tiny care detail,
- visual/emotional payoff.

Likely:
4–8 shots depending on pace.

Important:
do not spend several seconds on location establishment.

## Example B — 30-second casual date

Possible structure:
- meet / begin,
- choose destination or food,
- shared activity,
- playful beat,
- life-detail beat,
- care beat,
- quiet close.

Likely:
8–14 shots.

Use close reaction and detail shots to create density without adding many locations.

## Example C — 45-second city walk

Possible structure:
- close relational opening,
- walking and discovery,
- food/shop interaction,
- playful contrast,
- brief spatial reset,
- practical care,
- slower evening section,
- shared final view.

Likely:
12–20 shots.

Wide shots:
perhaps only location shift, one breathing reset, and final payoff.

## Example D — 60-second amusement/date day

Possible structure:
- arrival and playful entry,
- shared decision,
- activity 1,
- reaction peak,
- activity 2,
- playful transition,
- food/shopping detail,
- fatigue/rest,
- care beat,
- evening transition,
- quiet companionship,
- romantic payoff.

Likely:
16–26 shots.

Do not copy a fixed 18-shot pattern. Expand or compress according to actual actions and desired rhythm.

## Example E — 60-second home-life Vlog

Possible structure:
- waking/preparing fragment,
- making food together,
- small domestic disagreement,
- reaction/laughter,
- eating,
- cleanup or practical task,
- quiet rest,
- small act of care,
- evening shared activity,
- final calm detail.

The shot count may be lower than an outdoor activity Vlog because many moments benefit from longer observation.

## Example F — 90-second travel diary

Possible structure:
- departure,
- transport,
- arrival,
- first discovery,
- navigation,
- food,
- activity,
- fatigue,
- recovery/care,
- second location,
- sunset/night transition,
- reflective final sequence.

Likely:
22–40 shots.

Protect relationship readability. Do not let travel B-roll overwhelm the couple.
