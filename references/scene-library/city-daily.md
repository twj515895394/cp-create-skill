# Scene Library — City Daily

## Scope

Use for ordinary public urban life: streets, crossings, convenience stores, supermarkets, malls, cafés/restaurants, bookstores, cinemas, elevators, parking structures and neighborhood errands.

Route here when the urban system/place itself shapes behavior. Do not use it only because a city is visible in the background.

## Domain principle

City realism comes from:

- navigating shared public space,
- choosing and buying,
- waiting,
- carrying,
- reacting to crowds/signage,
- small transitions between destinations.

The city should create behavior.

---

## Street / crosswalk / sidewalk

### Realistic activity
- wait at signal,
- change walking pace,
- navigate around people,
- check direction,
- stop at storefront,
- cross together,
- step aside to check phone/map.

### Visual anchors
- crosswalk,
- traffic light,
- curb,
- storefront,
- sign,
- phone/map.

### Useful Motifs
- navigate,
- wait,
- discover,
- pace mismatch,
- environment interruption.

### Relationship affordances
- one walks ahead then checks back,
- one stops at a window,
- one handles navigation,
- both attention shifts to the same off-screen thing.

### AI-friendly notes
- crowds + traffic + exact faces can compound complexity,
- use medium follow for identity, wider back/side-back for urban scale.

## Convenience store

### Realistic activity
- enter and scan shelves,
- open fridge door,
- compare drinks/snacks,
- carry a small basket,
- change mind,
- checkout,
- open purchase outside.

### Visual anchors
- fridge doors,
- basket,
- snack packaging,
- receipt,
- checkout counter.

### Useful Motifs
- choose,
- discover,
- share,
- tiny disagreement,
- forgotten/extra item.

### Relationship affordances
- one adds an item the other did not expect,
- one waits at counter while the other runs back for something,
- one holds both drinks after checkout.

### State possibilities
- purchases become props for the next walking scene,
- receipt/bag marks completion.

### AI-friendly notes
- reflective fridge doors can complicate faces,
- favor product/hand inserts and clear ownership.

## Supermarket

### Realistic activity
- push cart,
- check list,
- compare brands/size,
- retrieve cold item,
- place items in cart,
- realize something was missed,
- bag purchases,
- divide heavy/light bags.

### Visual anchors
- cart,
- list/phone,
- shelves,
- produce bag,
- basket,
- checkout belt,
- shopping bags.

### Useful Motifs
- search,
- choose,
- divide task,
- forget/recover,
- practical care.

### Relationship affordances
- one pushes while the other fetches,
- one quietly removes/returns an unnecessary item,
- one takes heavier bag afterward.

### State possibilities
- empty cart → full cart,
- list items get checked off,
- bags carry into parking/home.

### AI-friendly notes
- cart geometry + shelves + crowds can be busy,
- medium side shots and object inserts are reliable,
- avoid both characters rapidly grabbing many small items simultaneously.

### Realistic activity
- ride escalator,
- browse windows,
- enter/leave stores,
- compare item,
- rest on bench,
- carry purchases,
- decide next floor/area.

### Visual anchors
- escalator,
- directory,
- shopping bag,
- fitting-room token,
- seating,
- storefront lighting.

### Useful Motifs
- discover,
- choose,
- wait,
- compare,
- recover.

### Relationship affordances
- one is distracted by storefront,
- one waits during try-on,
- shopping bags accumulate,
- route choice shifts based on fatigue.

### AI-friendly notes
- escalator and mirrors add complexity,
- identity-critical behavior works better on stable landing/bench/storefront shots.

## Café / casual restaurant

### Realistic activity
- read menu,
- order,
- wait,
- receive drinks/food,
- move items to make table space,
- taste,
- share,
- continue other activity,
- pay/collect belongings.

### Visual anchors
- menu,
- cup,
- receipt/order number,
- tray,
- napkin,
- table number,
- bag on chair/floor.

### Useful Motifs
- choose,
- wait,
- share,
- observe,
- rest.

### Relationship affordances
- one decides faster,
- one pushes a dish/drink across without pausing conversation,
- one notices the other finished a drink,
- quiet sitting can replace constant talking.

### State possibilities
- waiting → food arrives,
- cups empty,
- coats/bags shift from carried to placed.

### AI-friendly notes
- table scenes favor controlled medium/close shots,
- food-hand-mouth actions should not dominate every shot.

## Bookstore

### Realistic activity
- browse different shelves,
- pull book out,
- skim,
- show cover/page,
- lose track of partner briefly,
- regroup,
- carry one or two selections.

### Visual anchors
- shelves,
- book cover,
- tote/basket,
- reading table,
- signage.

### Useful Motifs
- discover,
- search,
- observe,
- share/recommend.

### Relationship affordances
- one becomes absorbed,
- the other finds them later,
- taste difference appears naturally.

### AI-friendly notes
- do not depend on readable book text,
- use book handling as object state, not text-recognition challenge.

## Cinema

### Realistic activity
- collect/check ticket,
- buy snacks,
- wait before entry,
- find seats,
- settle belongings,
- post-film reaction while exiting.

### Visual anchors
- ticket/QR,
- popcorn,
- cup,
- auditorium entrance,
- seat row,
- poster/lightbox.

### Useful Motifs
- wait,
- choose,
- share,
- synchronized attention,
- review/react afterward.

### Relationship affordances
- shared snack,
- one reacts more strongly after the film,
- pre/post viewing creates natural before/after structure.

### AI-friendly notes
- dark auditorium face identity is difficult,
- use pre-entry/post-exit face scenes and environment-focused interior shots.

## Elevator

### Realistic activity
- wait for doors,
- press button,
- step aside for others,
- enter,
- glance at floor display,
- exit quickly when doors open.

### Visual anchors
- button panel,
- floor display,
- mirrored wall,
- shopping bags.

### Useful Motifs
- wait,
- constrained silence,
- tiny timing mismatch.

### Relationship affordances
- one reaches past to press button,
- one holds door,
- close public-space distance can feel natural without overt romance.

### AI-friendly notes
- mirror duplication raises risk,
- short controlled shots are preferable.

- place bags,
- fold umbrella,
- load trunk,
- change shoes/jacket,
- check forgotten item,
- get in/out.

### Visual anchors
- key/fob,
- trunk,
- shopping bags,
- umbrella,
- parking column/sign.

### Useful Motifs
- search,
- finish,
- carry,
- weather transition.

### Relationship affordances
- one loads while the other holds items,
- heavy bags change hands,
- practical coordination closes an outing.

### AI-friendly notes
- doors/trunk + hands + bags can be mechanically complex,
- split loading detail from face reaction if needed.

---

## City Daily QC

Before using a City Daily scene:
- Does the public place create specific behavior?
- Are there useful systems/objects: signals, carts, menus, queues, doors, tickets, bags?
- Are several scenes secretly "walk and smile"?
- Can purchase/carrying state continue into later scenes?
- Is background complexity being separated from identity-critical shots where useful?
