# Scene Library — City Daily

## Scope

Use for ordinary public urban life: streets, convenience stores, supermarkets, malls, cafés, restaurants, cinemas, bookstores, elevators, parking structures and neighborhood errands.

## Subspace map

- Street / crossing — navigation, waiting signal, pace mismatch, window distraction.
- Convenience store — quick choice, fridge door, snack/drink, checkout, carrying.
- Supermarket — cart, list, shelf comparison, forgetting an item, bagging.
- Mall — browsing, escalator, fitting/trying, food court, rest.
- Café / restaurant — ordering, waiting, sharing, handling table objects, leaving.
- Cinema — ticket, snacks, waiting, post-view reaction.
- Bookstore — browsing, showing covers, losing track of partner, quiet observation.
- Elevator — brief waiting, button/door, mirror, confined-space silence.
- Parking — finding car, loading bags, umbrella/weather transition.

## Domain rule

Do not make the city only a backdrop. Use urban systems and objects to create behavior.

## AI notes

Busy crowds and reflective interiors raise complexity. Keep identity-critical interactions closer and simplify background demands when needed.
