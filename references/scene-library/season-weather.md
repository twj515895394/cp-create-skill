# Scene Library — Season / Weather

## Scope

Use when season or weather materially changes behavior: rain, snow, heat, wind, autumn, cold snap, summer night, sunset.

## Behavior map

- Rain — umbrella, wet clothing, shelter, slippery surfaces, wiping water.
- Snow — gloves, breath, footing, warming hands, snow accumulation.
- Heat — shade, drinks, sweat, slower pace, fan/AC transition.
- Wind — hair/clothes, holding items, changing walking direction.
- Autumn — leaves, layering, warm drinks, earlier light.
- Summer night — late outdoor food, insects, fan/breeze, ambient lights.

## Domain rule

Weather must cause visible behavior. Decoration alone is not enough.
