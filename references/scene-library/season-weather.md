# Scene Library — Season / Weather

## Scope

Use when **weather or seasonal conditions materially cause behavior**.

Examples:
- rain,
- snow,
- extreme heat,
- cold snap,
- strong wind,
- autumn conditions,
- summer night,
- sudden weather change,
- sunset/light-driven outdoor behavior.

Do not route here when weather is merely decorative.

"Rainy café date" is still City Daily if rain does not change what happens.
"Sudden rain forces them to share shelter / change route / deal with wet clothes" is Season / Weather.

## Domain principle

Weather should create:

- constraint,
- physical consequence,
- adaptation,
- state change,
- route/timing change,
- practical care.

---

## Rain

### Realistic activity
- open/close umbrella,
- avoid puddles,
- move under shelter,
- wipe wet phone/glasses/bag,
- shake/fold umbrella,
- change walking distance under one umbrella,
- remove wet outerwear indoors.

### Visual anchors
- umbrella,
- wet pavement,
- droplets,
- shelter edge,
- towel/tissue,
- damp jacket/hair.

### Useful Motifs
- environment interruption,
- share,
- care,
- recover,
- route change.

### State possibilities
- dry → wet,
- hair/clothing changes,
- umbrella becomes persistent prop,
- indoor transition becomes meaningful.

### AI-friendly notes
- rain + crowd + reflections + faces is high complexity,
- identity-critical shots can move under shelter or closer framing.

---

## Snow / cold

### Realistic activity
- put on gloves/hat,
- warm hands,
- shorten exposure,
- choose warmer route/space,
- brush snow off clothing,
- adjust scarf,
- step carefully.

### Visual anchors
- gloves,
- scarf,
- visible breath where appropriate,
- snow on clothing,
- hot drink.

### Useful Motifs
- care,
- recover,
- environment interruption,
- shared resource.

### State possibilities
- hands/face become cold,
- snow accumulates,
- hot drink/jacket gains functional meaning.

### AI-friendly notes
- snowfall can obscure details,
- use simple actions and clearer close shots for identity.

---

## Heat / strong sun

### Realistic activity
- seek shade,
- drink more often,
- wipe sweat,
- fan with hand/object,
- slow walking pace,
- move indoors,
- tie hair up/remove outer layer.

### Visual anchors
- bottle,
- shade line,
- handheld fan,
- sunglasses,
- towel/tissue.

### Useful Motifs
- recover,
- environment interruption,
- care,
- route adjustment.

### State possibilities
- fatigue increases,
- water decreases,
- clothing/hair changes for comfort.

---

## Wind

### Realistic activity
- hold hat/skirt/jacket/loose paper,
- turn body against wind,
- fix hair,
- secure bag,
- speak/gesture closer,
- abandon or relocate a light object setup.

### Visual anchors
- moving hair/clothes,
- paper/napkin,
- trees/flags,
- hat.

### Useful Motifs
- environment interruption,
- recover,
- shared task.

### AI-friendly notes
- wind already adds motion; simplify camera/body choreography if face consistency matters.

---

## Autumn / cooling weather

### Realistic activity
- add layer,
- warm drink,
- move between sun/shade,
- walk through leaves,
- brush leaves/debris from clothes,
- earlier evening transition.

### Visual anchors
- jacket,
- warm cup,
- leaves,
- lower-angle light.

### Useful Motifs
- observe,
- share,
- seasonal transition,
- quiet companionship.

### Rule
Autumn color alone is not enough to route here. Behavior/state must change.

---

## Summer night

### Realistic activity
- buy cold drink,
- sit outside later,
- fan away insects,
- move toward breeze,
- share late-night food,
- linger under ambient lights,
- walk more slowly after heat drops.

### Visual anchors
- convenience drink,
- fan,
- street lights,
- outdoor seating,
- insect repellent where appropriate.

### Useful Motifs
- recover,
- observe,
- share,
- quiet companionship.

### Routing note
If the main behavior is convenience-store/city dining, City Daily can remain Primary unless summer-night conditions meaningfully shape the experience.

---

## Sudden weather change

### Realistic activity
- notice first drops/wind,
- protect objects,
- change route,
- run/walk to shelter,
- reorganize bags,
- wait it out,
- continue with changed state.

### Useful Motifs
- discover,
- environment interruption,
- shared task,
- recover.

### Story value
Weather change is a strong natural micro-arc because it creates cause → adaptation → aftermath.

---

## Season / Weather QC

Before routing here:
- Does weather/season change what the characters physically do?
- Does it create state or consequence?
- Would the scene still be essentially identical in neutral weather?
  - If yes, keep the normal environment domain instead.
- Are weather effects adding too much AI complexity to face-critical shots?
