# Scene Library — Holidays / Special Days

## Scope

Use when **ritual, expectation, timing, preparation, gift/object meaning or memory** tied to a special day drives the behavior.

Examples:
- birthday,
- anniversary,
- New Year/countdown,
- Christmas/winter holiday,
- Spring Festival/family holiday,
- Valentine's Day,
- first night after moving,
- first trip day / last trip night,
- other personally meaningful milestone.

Do not route here merely because decorations are visible.

"Christmas mall shopping" is City Daily if it behaves like ordinary shopping.
It becomes Holidays / Special Days when gift preparation, timing, ritual or memory drives the event.

## Domain principle

Special-day content becomes believable through:

- preparation,
- anticipation,
- conceal/reveal,
- timing,
- ritual,
- objects gaining meaning,
- cleanup/aftermath,
- memory/callback.

Avoid a sequence of decorative poses.

## Birthday

### Realistic activity
- hide/prepare cake or gift,
- check timing,
- light candles,
- call the person over,
- small reveal,
- cut/share cake,
- open gift,
- clean up wrapping/plates.

### Visual anchors
- cake,
- candle,
- lighter/matches,
- gift bag/box,
- wrapping,
- card,
- plates.

### Useful Motifs
- prepare,
- wait,
- surprise,
- record,
- share,
- finish.

### State possibilities
- hidden gift → revealed object,
- intact cake → cut/partly eaten,
- wrapping accumulates,
- gift may carry into later scene.

### AI-friendly notes
- candles/fire + face + multiple hands can be complex,
- separate lighting detail from face reaction when useful.

---

## Anniversary

### Realistic activity
- revisit place/object,
- prepare small gift/note,
- look through old photo,
- repeat an old habit,
- compare then/now,
- save/keep a small object.

### Visual anchors
- photo,
- note/card,
- old ticket/object,
- gift,
- repeated location detail.

### Useful Motifs
- record/review,
- callback,
- prepare,
- quiet companionship.

### Story value
Anniversary content benefits from callbacks more than generic romantic gestures.

---

## New Year / countdown

### Realistic activity
- prepare snacks/drinks,
- check time,
- wait,
- move toward viewing area,
- countdown,
- react,
- send message/take quick photo,
- quiet aftermath after peak.

### Visual anchors
- clock/phone time,
- cups,
- lights,
- fireworks/confetti where appropriate.

### Useful Motifs
- wait,
- synchronized attention,
- payoff,
- record,
- aftermath.

### AI-friendly notes
- crowd + fireworks + exact faces is high load,
- use face reaction close, then environment wide/back.

## Christmas / winter holiday

### Realistic activity
- wrap gift,
- place ornament/light,
- choose gift,
- carry shopping bag,
- prepare warm drink/food,
- exchange/open gift,
- clean wrapping.

### Visual anchors
- wrapping paper,
- ribbon,
- lights,
- ornament,
- gift bag,
- warm drink.

### Useful Motifs
- prepare,
- choose,
- surprise,
- share,
- finish.

### Rule
Decorations alone do not create a holiday Scene Event.

---

## Spring Festival / family holiday

### Realistic activity
- prepare food,
- clean/arrange space,
- handle red envelopes/gifts where appropriate,
- travel/arrive,
- wait for meal,
- help serve/clear,
- late-night wind-down.

### Visual anchors
- food prep,
- bags/gifts,
- table setting,
- holiday decoration used functionally.

### Routing note
If family/travel logistics dominate, Travel or Shared Tasks can be Primary. Use this domain when ritual/holiday expectation is what shapes the behavior.

---

## Valentine's / couple-specific special day

### Realistic activity
- prepare small object/note,
- decide where to go,
- hide/hand over gift,
- react,
- carry/use the object later,
- end with ordinary shared time rather than continuous staged romance.

### Useful Motifs
- prepare,
- surprise,
- share,
- callback.

---

## First night after moving

### Realistic activity
- unpack only essentials,
- sit among boxes,
- eat simple takeaway,
- find charger/blanket,
- improvise because furniture is incomplete,
- clean minimal area before sleep.

### Visual anchors
- boxes,
- tape,
- takeaway,
- floor seating,
- charger,
- blanket.

### Useful Motifs
- shared task,
- recover,
- discover,
- quiet companionship.

### Routing note
If moving logistics/assembly dominate, Shared Tasks is Primary. Use Special Days when the emotional significance of "first night here" drives the scene.

---

## First trip day / last trip night

### Realistic activity
- first arrival ritual,
- first room/window reveal,
- last-night repacking,
- review photos,
- check souvenirs,
- quiet fatigue,
- prepare early departure.

### Routing note
Travel remains Primary when journey logistics dominate. Use Special Days only when the milestone meaning itself is the creative focus.

---

## Holidays / Special Days QC

Before routing here:
- Is the special-day ritual/expectation actually causing behavior?
- Are meaningful objects/timing/state involved?
- Would the scene work unchanged on an ordinary day?
  - If yes, route to the normal environment domain.
- Is the story relying on decoration instead of action?
- Can the ending pay off an object, ritual or callback established earlier?
