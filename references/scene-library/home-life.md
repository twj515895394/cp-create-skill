# Scene Library — Home Life

## Scope

Use for ordinary life inside a home: bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, dining area, living room, balcony, study, laundry and domestic transitions.

## Subspace map

- Bedroom — waking, getting dressed, making bed, looking for items, winding down.
- Bathroom / vanity — washing, brushing teeth, skincare, hair, mirror routines.
- Kitchen — preparation, cooking, tasting, cleaning as part of daily life.
- Dining — serving, eating, leftovers, clearing dishes.
- Living room — lounging, TV, games, reading, phone, casual conversation, naps.
- Balcony / window — laundry, plants, weather, quiet observation.
- Entryway — shoes, keys, bags, leaving/returning.
- Laundry / storage — folding, sorting, searching, putting away.

## Domain rule

Home-life realism comes from unfinished actions, shared objects, habit and comfortable asymmetry.

Use Mother Motifs and Micro Moments only when routed; do not import their full libraries automatically.

## AI notes

Home spaces support stable close/medium observational shots. Avoid adding complex camera movement when the value is in small hand/body behavior.
