# Micro Moments — Holidays / Special Days

## Purpose

Small ritual behaviors tied to timing, gifts, preparation, reveal, memory and aftermath.

Use only when the special-day meaning is part of the Scene Event.

## Gift preparation

- hide bag/box out of direct view,
- remove price tag/receipt,
- smooth wrapping paper edge,
- retie ribbon after it loosens,
- keep card face-down until the right moment,
- move gift from obvious place when partner approaches.

## Timing / anticipation

- check phone time once or twice,
- lower voice while preparing nearby,
- light candles only shortly before reveal,
- pause at door before calling partner in,
- keep one hand behind back or near hidden object only when natural.

## Reveal / reaction

- recipient looks at giver before object,
- gift is turned/inspected before opening fully,
- wrapping is placed aside rather than vanishing,
- card is read after initial object reaction,
- opened item remains nearby and can carry into later scenes.

## Cake / food ritual

- lighter/match is put down before applause/reaction,
- candle wax/flame state is kept simple,
- plates/knife appear only when cutting begins,
- first slice is slightly imperfect,
- napkins/plates accumulate afterward.

## Countdown / fireworks

- phone time is checked before countdown,
- attention shifts from each other to the same external event,
- reaction becomes quieter after the peak,
- one quick photo/video may be taken, then phone lowers,
- the final wide may show backs/silhouettes rather than distant face identity.

## Cleanup / aftermath

- ribbon/wrapping is gathered,
- candles/plates are cleared,
- gift packaging stays visible for a while,
- tired posture appears after hosting/preparing,
- meaningful object is placed somewhere deliberate instead of disappearing.

## Use rule

Special-day Micro Moments should create **ritual continuity and object memory**, not decorative busyness.
