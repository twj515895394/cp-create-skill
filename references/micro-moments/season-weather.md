# Micro Moments — Season / Weather

## Purpose

Small behaviors caused by weather or seasonal conditions.

Use only when weather materially affects the Scene Event.

## Rain

- umbrella is opened before stepping out from cover,
- wet umbrella is held away from clothing indoors,
- droplets are wiped from phone/glasses,
- one person steps around a puddle and changes line,
- damp hair is pushed away from face,
- sleeve/shoulder is brushed after rain,
- umbrella is shaken/folded near entrance rather than instantly disappearing.

## Snow / cold

- hands briefly warm inside sleeves/pockets,
- gloves are removed only for a precise phone/object action,
- snow is brushed from shoulder/hair,
- scarf is tightened after wind picks up,
- hot cup is held with both hands,
- walking pace shortens on slippery ground.

## Heat

- bottle is opened more often,
- shade is chosen before sitting,
- sweat is wiped before touching phone,
- hair is tied back or moved off neck,
- outer layer is removed/carrying state changes,
- pace slows naturally,
- cold drink is held against wrist/neck briefly only when plausible.

## Wind

- one hand secures hat/loose paper,
- hair is fixed repeatedly but not every second,
- bag strap is tightened,
- napkin/package is pinned with an object,
- body angle turns slightly against wind,
- conversation distance narrows because sound is harder to hear.

## Seasonal transition

- jacket is put on only after temperature drops,
- warm drink becomes carried prop,
- light change prompts leaving/returning,
- fallen leaves/sand/snow are brushed from clothing before entering indoors.

## Use rule

Weather Micro Moments must be **causal**.

If the same detail would happen in neutral conditions, route it to the normal domain instead.
