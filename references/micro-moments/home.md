# Micro Moments — Home

## Purpose

Small domestic behaviors that make a concrete Home-Life Scene Event feel inhabited rather than posed.

Use 1–3 details when they support state, habit, continuity or relationship.

## Waking / bedroom

- silence alarm without fully opening eyes,
- sit at bed edge before standing,
- feel around for slippers,
- pull blanket back into place after getting up,
- retrieve phone from charger,
- unplug cable with one hand while holding phone in the other,
- search bedside area before realizing item is elsewhere,
- place worn clothing temporarily on chair rather than immediately organizing it,
- close/open curtain after first checking the light.

## Bathroom / vanity

- squeeze excess water from hands before reaching for towel,
- move an item aside to make counter space,
- reach around partner for toothbrush/skincare,
- wipe a small splash from counter,
- hold hair away while washing face,
- unplug and coil hair-dryer cord,
- put used towel back imperfectly,
- inspect face/hair briefly after finishing rather than staring continuously at mirror.

## Kitchen

- push sleeve up before washing/cutting,
- use hip/elbow to close cabinet/fridge when hands are occupied,
- taste from spoon and pause before deciding seasoning,
- set utensil down on the same small rest/plate,
- wipe fingertips before touching phone,
- move a bowl aside to make cutting space,
- return milk/ingredient to fridge mid-process,
- pick up dropped packaging/peel while waiting for water/pan,
- hand over one ingredient without turning it into a pose.

## Dining

- rotate bowl/plate slightly before eating,
- move phone or bag to create table space,
- pass dish while continuing to eat,
- refill/shift cup,
- wipe mouth/fingers before touching shared item,
- leave one disliked ingredient,
- stack small dishes after finishing,
- collect cups/plates in two trips rather than magically clearing everything.

## Living room / sofa

- remote disappears under blanket/cushion and gets searched for,
- snack bowl shifts between people,
- charger cable limits where phone can move,
- one person adjusts sitting posture gradually,
- blanket is pulled only partly across the other person,
- one continues watching screen while accepting snack/drink,
- pause content to answer door/phone then resume,
- put feet down before standing to get something.

## Entryway

- check pocket after shoes are already on,
- place keys in tray then pick them back up,
- balance briefly while putting on shoe,
- move bag to opposite shoulder,
- return two steps for forgotten phone/item,
- hold door while other finishes shoes,
- fold wet umbrella before bringing it farther inside.

## Laundry / clothing

- turn clothing right-side-out before folding,
- pair socks and discover one missing,
- shake garment once before hanging,
- move folded stack to free basket,
- carry basket against hip,
- remove hanger with one hand while holding garment,
- put one item aside because it belongs elsewhere.

## Bedtime

- place water near bed,
- plug phone in and test connection,
- set alarm then lower screen brightness,
- close curtain partly, then adjust it again,
- move pillow before lying down,
- pull blanket into place after settling,
- reach once more for phone/book before lights-out.

## Use rule

Home Micro Moments should reveal **habit and shared-space familiarity**.

Avoid making both characters constantly perform tiny gestures at the same time.
