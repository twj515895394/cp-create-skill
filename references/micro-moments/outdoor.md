# Micro Moments — Outdoor

## Purpose

Small physical behaviors caused by terrain, open-air conditions, equipment and accumulated effort.

Use only after the Outdoor Scene Event is concrete.

## Park / walking

- pace changes around uneven path or other people,
- bottle/tote switches hands,
- one person pauses at shade/bench before the other,
- bag is placed down before sitting on grass,
- clothing is brushed after standing from lawn,
- one person turns back after walking slightly ahead.

## Picnic

- blanket corner is flattened or weighted,
- container lid is placed under/next to container instead of disappearing,
- cup is moved away from unstable edge,
- napkin is used to wipe fingers before phone,
- food packaging is gathered into one bag,
- items are repacked in a different order than arrival.

## Cycling

- helmet strap is checked once,
- seat/bag is adjusted before starting,
- feet reposition on pedal after stop,
- one person slows at crossing and waits,
- bike is parked before conversation/reaction,
- water is taken only after stopping.

## Lakeside / riverside

- hair/clothing reacts to breeze,
- hand rests briefly on rail while looking out,
- drink is set down carefully on uneven surface,
- one person points once, then both keep watching,
- jacket is added/removed as temperature shifts.

## Beach

- sandals are carried after shoes come off,
- feet hesitate at cold water,
- sand is shaken from towel/clothing,
- wet hem is lifted/adjusted,
- hair is moved away repeatedly because of wind,
- belongings are checked before leaving shoreline.

## Camping

- gear bag remains open while setup is incomplete,
- tent pole/strap is held steady for partner,
- lantern/flashlight is repositioned after dark,
- chair is unfolded before sitting,
- cooler/container lid is closed after use,
- setup debris/packaging is collected before relaxing.

## Trail / hike

- backpack strap is tightened after incline,
- one person waits at a wider spot on trail,
- bottle is returned to same pocket/side,
- shoe lace/fit is checked at rest,
- breathing remains heavier for a few seconds after stopping,
- sweat/dust is wiped before taking phone/photo.

## Pack-down / leaving

- trash is collected,
- blanket/towel is shaken before folding,
- bags become fuller/more irregular,
- equipment is counted/check visually,
- last person looks back at spot before leaving only when motivated.

## Use rule

Outdoor Micro Moments should reveal **terrain, effort or environment**.

Do not duplicate Weather Micro Moments unless weather is actually the main cause.
