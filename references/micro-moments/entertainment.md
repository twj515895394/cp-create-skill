# Micro Moments — Entertainment

## Theme park / attraction

### Entry / navigation
- ticket or phone remains in hand for a moment after scanning,
- map is opened too wide, then folded smaller,
- one finger traces the route while the other person follows it with their eyes,
- bag strap is adjusted before moving off,
- one person takes two steps, then turns back when the other is still reading.

### Queue
- weight shifts from one leg to the other,
- hand rests on railing,
- glance toward ride, then back to partner,
- phone is checked briefly and put away,
- drink/snack gradually gets finished,
- one person advances with the line and the other notices a beat later.

### Boarding
- sit and pull clothing/bag clear before restraint closes,
- check restraint with both hands,
- quick glance to partner immediately before dispatch,
- shoulders tense before movement starts.

### Post-ride
- push hair back into place,
- exhale/laugh before speaking,
- walk more slowly for several steps,
- compare reactions without stopping,
- reach for water,
- check whether belongings are all present.

### Snack / food
- open packaging while walking slowly,
- hold bucket/cup against body to free one hand,
- steal a small bite when partner looks elsewhere,
- wipe fingers on napkin,
- pass container without turning the moment into a pose.

### Shopping
- lift item, inspect, put back, then return to it,
- use mirror for a quick try-on,
- shopping bag changes hands later,
- receipt/ticket is tucked into the bag,
- one person keeps browsing while the other waits nearby.

### Rest
- bags placed between feet or beside bench,
- knees/feet reposition after sitting,
- bottle cap loosened before handing over,
- map/phone checked while drinking,
- standing up happens reluctantly after a pause.

### Show / parade / fireworks
- adjust standing position to see past crowd,
- rise slightly on toes,
- point briefly then lower hand,
- both attention lines converge on the same off-screen event,
- face reaction appears before the environment payoff,
- after the spectacle starts, interaction becomes quieter rather than more performative.

### Zoo / animal encounter
- passenger leans forward or lifts binoculars / camera when movement appears beyond the vehicle,
- point briefly across the windshield, then lower the hand once the partner has found the target,
- driver keeps route control while following the passenger's cue,
- both turn toward the same side, then return to the forward view as the animal passes,
- hold a quieter shared look for a beat after a near pass before moving on.

## Use rule

Entertainment Micro Moments should create the feeling of **spending time at the venue**, not merely visiting headline attractions.
