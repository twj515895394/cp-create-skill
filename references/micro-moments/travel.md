# Micro Moments — Travel

## Purpose

Small behaviors that show accumulated travel state: luggage, documents, waiting, fatigue, charging, weather and temporary living.

## Packing / departure

- charger is wrapped, then reopened because it is needed,
- suitcase zipper pauses at an overfilled corner,
- small item is moved from suitcase to day bag,
- pockets are checked after shoes/coat are already on,
- suitcase handle is extended only when leaving,
- one person carries loose item while the other controls luggage.

## Airport / station movement

- suitcase handle switches hands after a long walk,
- one person stops and pulls luggage slightly to the side before checking board,
- ticket/phone is brought out before reaching gate,
- bag strap is shortened/tightened,
- passport/ID is returned to the same pocket after use,
- luggage wheels catch briefly on threshold/turn,
- one person walks ahead to read signs then slows for the other.

## Security / control points

- small items are gathered into one tray/hand,
- jacket is put back on after moving away from checkpoint,
- phone/wallet are checked before continuing,
- bag zipper is closed again after retrieving document,
- belongings are reorganized rather than instantly returning to perfect state.

## Gate / platform waiting

- luggage stands between feet or beside seat,
- phone charges with cable stretched toward outlet,
- one person watches bags while the other gets water/coffee,
- cup is set on suitcase handle tray/nearby surface,
- boarding info is checked more than once,
- posture changes from alert to tired,
- standing up begins before final belongings are fully gathered.

## Onboard

- bag is pushed under seat/stowed before sitting fully,
- jacket is folded loosely over lap/seat,
- tray table is lowered/raised,
- headphones are untangled,
- window shade is adjusted,
- one person sleeps while the other keeps screen brightness low,
- bottle/cup is stabilized before vehicle movement,
- seat area is checked before getting off.

## Arrival / baggage

- eyes follow carousel before bag appears,
- one person recognizes luggage first,
- suitcase is pulled off and rolled aside before handle is extended,
- coat/jacket is adjusted for local weather,
- phone map is opened only after clearing the flow of people,
- bags are counted once before leaving.

## Hotel room entry

- key card fails or is tried again without turning it into a gag,
- suitcase is rolled just inside before room exploration,
- lights are tested,
- curtain is opened early,
- one person looks at view while the other moves luggage,
- shoes/coat are removed progressively,
- room card is placed in a consistent spot,
- outlet is searched for near bed/table.

## Unpacking / temporary living

- only frequently needed items come out,
- charger immediately occupies a visible outlet,
- toiletries move toward bathroom,
- worn clothing gets its own corner/bag,
- suitcase remains partly open,
- small hotel items are shifted to make personal space,
- water/kettle is prepared while another task continues.

## Return fatigue

- packing is less neat than departure,
- drawers/outlets are checked for forgotten items,
- charger is one of the last things removed,
- souvenirs make bag harder to close,
- walking pace is slower,
- seated waiting becomes longer/quieter,
- luggage is dropped temporarily near home entry before full unpacking.

## Use rule

Travel Micro Moments should make the viewer feel that **time, weight and fatigue accumulated**.

Do not reset luggage, clothing and energy to a pristine state every scene.
