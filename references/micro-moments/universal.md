# Micro Moments — Universal

## Purpose

Reusable low-level behaviors that can appear across many environments.

Use selectively. These are not mandatory animation beats.

## Object handling

- switch a bag/object from one hand to the other,
- temporarily tuck phone into pocket/bag,
- place carried items down before sitting,
- collect the same items again before leaving,
- twist a bottle cap before passing the bottle,
- fold or refold a paper/map,
- hold an item against the body while freeing one hand,
- check pockets before moving on,
- use one hand to stabilize an object while the other opens it.

## Clothing / appearance

- push sleeve up before using hands,
- straighten a collar/hem after sitting,
- brush lint/crumbs from clothing,
- move loose hair away from face,
- fix wind-messy hair after movement,
- adjust bag strap when it slips,
- tug clothing back into place after standing,
- remove/put on an outer layer because temperature changes.

## Body state

- shift weight while waiting,
- slow pace after exertion,
- briefly stretch neck/shoulder,
- rub hands when cold,
- wipe sweat or moisture,
- flex or rub tired feet/legs,
- take a deeper breath after exertion,
- sit first, then settle into a more relaxed posture,
- stand with a slight delay after resting.

## Attention

- look at object first, then partner,
- continue watching the environment while accepting an item,
- glance back to check whether partner is following,
- notice partner only after finishing a small task,
- briefly track a sound/movement off-screen,
- read/check something twice before deciding.

## Shared-space behavior

- hold a door while continuing movement,
- make space on a bench/table,
- move bags to free a seat,
- pass behind/around partner rather than teleporting positions,
- wait half a beat while partner finishes an action,
- match walking pace after briefly drifting apart.

## Use rule

Prefer details that reflect the current state.

Do not add random fidgeting just to create motion.
