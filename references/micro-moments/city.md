# Micro Moments — City

## Purpose

Small public-space behaviors for concrete City Daily scenes.

Use them to show navigation, carrying, buying, waiting and social-space adjustment.

## Street / crosswalk

- slow at curb before signal changes,
- step slightly aside to check phone/map,
- one person walks half a step ahead then matches pace,
- glance at storefront and drift toward it,
- move closer to edge of sidewalk to let others pass,
- adjust bag after crossing,
- turn head toward traffic before stepping out,
- hold takeaway cup lower while navigating a crowd.

## Convenience store

- fridge door held with forearm while choosing,
- take one drink, compare label/size, put one back,
- basket switches hands,
- snack is added at the last second near checkout,
- receipt is folded and tucked into bag/pocket,
- plastic/paper bag handles are gathered into one hand,
- purchase opened just outside rather than at shelf.

## Supermarket

- one hand stays on cart while the other checks list,
- stop cart slightly to the side before browsing shelf,
- item goes into cart, then is replaced with another version,
- produce bag opening takes a second attempt,
- cold item is picked near the end and placed separately,
- one person pushes cart forward while the other catches up with an item,
- bags are redistributed after checkout by weight,
- receipt/list is checked before leaving.

## Café / restaurant

- bag placed on empty chair/floor before sitting,
- menu is turned slightly toward partner,
- cup is moved to make room for arriving plate,
- napkin is pulled only when needed,
- straw/lid is adjusted,
- one person keeps looking at menu while the other is already ready,
- finished cup is nudged aside,
- belongings are collected in a small sequence before standing.

## Mall / escalator

- shopping bag changes hands,
- one person steps onto escalator first and turns slightly,
- pause at directory before choosing direction,
- one stops at display while the other walks one or two steps ahead,
- bench rest begins with bags placed at feet,
- jacket is taken off/put on when moving between indoor/outdoor temperature.

## Bookstore

- finger stays between pages while briefly showing a book,
- book is returned carefully to same shelf,
- tote/basket rests against leg while browsing,
- one person reads longer than expected while the other shifts position/waits,
- selected book is carried against chest/under arm.

## Cinema

- ticket/phone is prepared before reaching scanner,
- popcorn bucket gets repositioned to free one hand,
- cup goes into holder before sitting,
- jacket/bag is moved off seat,
- after film, one person stretches before walking out,
- reaction begins while still exiting rather than as a staged stop-and-talk.

## Elevator

- button pressed once, then hand drops,
- step aside when doors open for others,
- shopping bags are pulled closer to body,
- glance at floor display,
- one person reaches to hold door for the other,
- exit happens quickly when correct floor opens.

## Parking / loading

- car key/fob searched in bag/pocket,
- trunk opens while bags are temporarily placed down,
- heavy bag lifted with two hands,
- umbrella is shaken/folded before loading,
- trolley/cart is returned or moved aside,
- last check around trunk before closing.

## Use rule

City Micro Moments should reflect **public-space friction and object handling**, not random fidgeting.
