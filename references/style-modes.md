# Supported Content Modes

The skill is Vlog-first, but it supports adjacent couple-content directions.

## 1. Daily Sweet Vlog — default

Primary characteristics:
- intimate framing,
- natural behavior,
- real-life details,
- playful chemistry,
- small acts of care,
- light narrative arc,
- low dependence on dialogue,
- close shots dominant.

Best for:
- everyday dates,
- weekend outings,
- casual city life,
- food and shopping,
- simple shared activities.

## 2. Date-Day Vlog

More event-driven than daily Vlog.

Use:
- preparation,
- meeting,
- destination,
- activity sequence,
- small surprise,
- rest,
- evening payoff.

Keep the relationship more important than the venue.

## 3. Travel Couple Diary

Adds:
- transportation,
- discovery,
- navigation,
- local details,
- fatigue and recovery,
- weather,
- arrival/departure motifs.

Risk:
becoming a tourism montage.

Countermeasure:
for every major location beat, include a relationship or reaction beat nearby.

## 4. Home-Life Diary

Adds:
- routine,
- tactile details,
- food,
- chores,
- pets,
- shared objects,
- pauses.

Camera language:
even closer and quieter than outdoor Vlog.

Strong themes:
comfort, familiarity, teamwork, domestic humor.

## 5. Seasonal / Holiday Memory

Examples:
- summer night,
- autumn walk,
- winter lights,
- Christmas,
- New Year,
- birthday.

Use recurring seasonal motifs to unify the piece.

Do not let decorations replace the couple story.

## 6. Anniversary / Memory Film

More reflective and emotionally designed.

Can include:
- motifs,
- objects with meaning,
- repeated locations,
- time contrast,
- voiceover,
- longer closing shots.

Still retain authentic behavior.

## 7. Observational Couple Diary

Feels almost documentary.

Characteristics:
- less posing,
- more static observation,
- more ambient sound,
- incomplete actions,
- off-camera moments,
- natural imperfection.

Best when realism matters more than polish.

## 8. Light Narrative Micro-Film

A small scripted situation with Vlog intimacy.

Examples:
- missed train becomes an unexpected date,
- searching for a specific shop,
- preparing a surprise,
- one person secretly documenting the day.

Use stronger causality than standard Vlog, but avoid turning every shot into formal cinema coverage.

## 9. Future expansion directions

The architecture can later support:
- relationship comedy,
- romantic micro-drama,
- breakup/reunion memory pieces,
- long-distance couple diary,
- proposal/engagement films,
- wedding-adjacent couple stories,
- fictional CP fan-content formats.

These should be added deliberately rather than mixed into the default Vlog behavior.
